How to structure a legal website around case types. For attorneys, the problem is rarely a shortage of marketing terminology. It is the distance between an online search, a useful answer, a verified professional, and an intake conversation that could become an appropriate engagement. This resource examines seo for law firms as an operating discipline instead of a quick ranking trick. It is intended for partners, practice managers, and law firm marketing teams weighing where to place limited time and budget. The examples are illustrative and do not promise a particular ranking, lead count, or business result.

Separate practice-area demand

A homepage can introduce a practice, but it should not be the only document competing for a truck, motorcycle, premises or wrongful-death query. Each substantive matter needs its own destination.

The strategic risk is choosing an attractive metric instead of a useful objective. Build logical internal pathways

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

Applying this to a real decision

Consider a regional firm with car, truck and motorcycle injury teams. In this illustrative situation, a broad injury page receives impressions while specialist searches land on irrelevant pages. A defensible next action would be to establish separate matter hubs with attorney evidence, internal pathways and clear eligibility information. This does not substitute for a complete discovery process; it creates a bounded test with a real owner.

The team should write down the baseline before deploying changes. Capture which landing page or channel receives the inquiry, how that inquiry is assessed, and which follow-up action is scheduled. Where professional obligations apply, have the responsible attorney approve sensitive language. The useful output is a decision record: what changed, what evidence supports the change, and what remains uncertain. It is possible for a technically successful campaign to underperform commercially if the practice cannot respond to the right opportunities.

DECISION SUPPORT

Frequently asked questions

Should a law firm have one page for every city?

Only where the firm can support real local relevance. A page must contain useful office, attorney, service and community information; dozens of interchangeable location pages risk poor user value.

Can several practice areas share one page?

Related areas can share an overview, but complex case types usually need dedicated pages that answer distinct liability and evidence questions.

How are location and matter pages linked?

Use relevant contextual links that guide visitors from a city overview to a matter-specific page and from matter pages to actual attorneys and consultation options.
